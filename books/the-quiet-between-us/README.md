# The Quiet Between Us

The 31-page expanded art edition by Orion Saint, with seven paintings by Ivaylo Peytchev.

Public route: https://ivosart.com/books/the-quiet-between-us/

The PDF is the approved illustrated edition. `pages/01.webp` through `pages/31.webp` are faithful 900 × 1260 renders of that PDF; do not rewrite or repaginate the manuscript when updating the reader. The complete poem transcript is in the HTML for accessible and script-free reading.

The reader uses self-hosted StPageFlip 2.0.7 (`assets/vendor/page-flip-2.0.7.js`), distributed under the accompanying MIT license. Source: https://github.com/Nodlik/StPageFlip ; distribution: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/page-flip@2.0.7/dist/js/page-flip.browser.js . It has no runtime dependencies or CDN requests.

Features: responsive spreads, single-page mode, corner dragging, swiping, previous/next and keyboard navigation, contents selection, image zoom, PDF download, reduced-motion navigation, page fragments, and local reading-position storage. Reading-position storage contains only a page number and never leaves the visitor's device.

If JavaScript is unavailable, pages remain horizontally scrollable and both the text edition and PDF remain usable. The original homepage, collection, artwork pages and APIs are preserved. `scripts/build-catalogue.js` retains the gallery link and sitemap entry during future regeneration.
