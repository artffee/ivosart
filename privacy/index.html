Your inquiry. Your information.

When you contact IvosArt, your name, email address, artwork of interest, and message are used to respond to your inquiry and discuss the artwork or project with you. Contact: usivaylo@gmail.com.

Sending your message

If the form offers “Open email draft,” your message is prepared in your own email application and is sent only when you send that email. “Copy inquiry” copies the text to your device’s clipboard.

If direct delivery is enabled and the button says “Send inquiry,” the details are transmitted through the website’s server and Resend, its email delivery provider, to the studio inbox. They are not displayed publicly by the website. Vercel hosts the website and may process technical request information as part of providing hosting and security.

No newsletter signup

Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails. Avoid including payment card information, passwords, or other sensitive information in the form or the A305X chat.

The A305X guide

The optional A305X guide and playful art reader use Google’s Gemini service when available. Messages and selected reading inputs may be sent to that service to generate a response. The artwork-reading game can also use a local fallback. Neither feature needs your contact details.

Questions or requests

Contact the studio to ask about information in your inquiry, request a correction, or request deletion. Third-party service records are subject to their own policies.
