Meet Ivo · Sofia to South Florida

A life spent making.

Born in Sofia and based in South Florida, Ivaylo Peytchev is a Bulgarian artist, writer, and maker. IvosArt brings together his original paintings, works on paper, and art statements.

His painting follows movement: colour dragged, thrown, and allowed to find its own order. Some works are loud with colour; others say more with a single line.

Beyond the canvas, he writes as Orion Saint and creates the playful worlds of A305X and NEMO Studio. For a painting, a commission, or a studio viewing, speak with Ivo directly.

“The hand never lies. Whatever happened in front of the canvas, the paint remembers.”
Ivaylo Peytchev

Know every detail.

Agree the artwork, final price, framing, and delivery with the studio before making a payment.

Is every work an original?

The original-work collection includes paintings and works on paper. Framed statements and collage studies are presented separately; their available formats, edition details, and production options are confirmed on inquiry.

Can I commission something for my space?

Send your preferred colours, approximate size, location, and any timing requirements. Ivo will discuss the idea and confirm whether he can take it on.

Is the frame included? Can you ship to me?

Frames pictured are part of the presentation. Ask the studio to confirm what is included, the final dimensions, and delivery options and costs for your location.

Can I see the painting before deciding?

Ask for more photographs, close-up details, or a private viewing by appointment. Availability will be confirmed by the studio.
